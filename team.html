Started on a horse farm

Dana grew up off Highway 326 watching her family fight the same losing battle every summer: thirsty grass, brown patches, and a water bill that kept climbing.

After 12 years at a wholesale native nursery she opened Fern & Field to help homeowners do it differently. We still grow most of our natives on site, and we will always tell you when the cheaper option is the right one.
