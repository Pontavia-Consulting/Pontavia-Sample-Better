Why your St. Augustine dies every April

April is the driest month in Marion County, and turf grass on deep sand needs more water than our watering rules allow. Here is what to plant instead, and how to transition one section at a time.

Start small. Pick the sunniest, hardest-to-water corner of the yard, strip the turf, and plant a group of three to five of the same plant. Water every other day for two weeks, then twice a week until the rainy season starts.

Once that patch looks good, do the next one. Most of our clients convert a front yard over two or three seasons without ever living in a construction zone.
